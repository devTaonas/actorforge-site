Companies House API Scraper: UK Company Directors, SIC Codes and Filings

One fully enriched row per UK company: registered details, SIC codes, directors and officers, and the 25 most recent filings, from the official register.

Run on Apify →See pricing

What it does

The Companies House website is fine for looking up one company. It is slow when you need two hundred, and the official API makes you stitch together separate calls for the profile, the officers and the filing history, then deal with rate limits.

Companies House Enrichment does that stitching for you. Give it a list of company numbers, a free-text name search, or advanced filters (SIC codes, status, incorporation date range, registered-office location) and it returns one row per company with the registered address, status, incorporation date, SIC codes, current and resigned officers (name, role, appointment and resignation dates, occupation, nationality) and the 25 most recent filings (date, category, type, description). Data comes from the official Companies House API, so it is as current as the register itself.

It suits lead generation ('active scaffolding companies in Manchester incorporated since 2020'), KYC and supplier checks, and bulk enrichment of company numbers already in a spreadsheet. Privacy is built in: officers' dates of birth and personal addresses are never returned, only statutory business-register fields.

How it works

  1. If you pass companyNumbers, those exact companies are enriched; otherwise the actor runs an advanced search on SIC code, status, incorporation dates and location, or a plain name search with searchTerms.
  2. For each company it calls the official Companies House API for the profile, then (if enabled) the officers list and the filing history.
  3. Responses are merged into one clean row; officers are capped at 100 per company and filings at the 25 most recent.
  4. Requests stay within the Companies House rate limit of 600 requests per 5 minutes (a fully enriched company uses 3), and you can add your own free API key for dedicated throughput.
  5. Mistyped or missing company numbers are skipped and never charged, and the run stops cleanly at maxResults or your spend limit.

FAQ

How do I get a list of directors for many UK companies at once?

Put the company numbers into companyNumbers and keep includeOfficers on. Each output row then has an officers list with name, role, appointment and resignation dates, occupation and nationality, up to 100 officers per company.

Can I search Companies House by SIC code and incorporation date?

Yes. Use sicCodes (for example 43991 for scaffolding) with companyStatus, incorporatedFrom, incorporatedTo and location. These advanced filters take over from searchTerms, and companyNumbers takes priority over both.

Does it return directors' dates of birth or home addresses?

No. Dates of birth and personal addresses are never returned. You get only statutory business-register fields: name, role, dates, occupation and nationality.

Which companies are covered?

Companies on the official Companies House register: UK-registered companies in England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland. Overseas companies not on the register are not covered.

Do I need my own Companies House API key?

No, a shared key is used by default. You can add your own free key from developer.company-information.service.gov.uk in apiKey for dedicated throughput of 600 requests per 5 minutes, which is roughly 180 fully enriched companies per 5 minutes.

How much does it cost to enrich 1,000 companies?

$5.00, at $0.005 per enriched company with profile, officers and filings included. 100 companies cost $0.50. Companies that are not found are never charged.
