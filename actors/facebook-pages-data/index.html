Facebook Business Pages by City: Page URLs, Phones and Emails

One row per Facebook Business Page in a city: page link and ID, contacts and location, from open data, without ever visiting facebook.com.

Run on Apify →See pricing

What it does

Social media agencies and local marketers often want a simple list: which restaurants, gyms or salons in this city have a Facebook Page, and how do I reach them? Getting that from Facebook itself means logins, cookies, proxies and an account at risk, and the Graph API will not hand you pages by location.

This actor answers the question from a different direction. In the Overture Maps places dataset, Meta contributes its business places under CDLA-Permissive-2.0, so most businesses carry their Facebook Page link. The actor searches a city, bounding box or radius, keeps only places that link a real Page, normalises the URL and parses out the numeric page ID (or vanity handle), and returns the business name, category, address, coordinates, phone, website, business email and other social links. In central Lisbon, 24,243 places at confidence 0.6 or more link a Facebook Page (23,718 unique pages).

It is not a Facebook scraper and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by Meta. You get contact and location data, not page stats: no likes, followers, posts, ads, reviews or about text, and you cannot feed it a list of page URLs to look up. If that fits your workflow, it is fast, blocks nothing and costs $4 per 1,000 pages.

How it works

  1. You choose an area (city name, bbox or lat/lon plus radius) and optional categories such as restaurant, gym or dentist.
  2. The actor reads the matching part of the newest monthly Overture places release and keeps places whose links include a Facebook Page.
  3. Page links in many formats (numeric ids, vanity handles, /pages/ URLs, profile.php?id=, mobile hosts) are normalised and parsed into facebookPageId or pageHandle; groups, events and share links are skipped for free.
  4. When several places point to the same page, only the highest-confidence one is returned, so you get one row per page.
  5. Phones are normalised to E.164, personal-looking emails are removed, and each row carries sources, licence and attribution.

Limits and honest notes

  • Not Meta data via Facebook: the rows come from Overture Maps open data. There are no likes, followers, check-ins, ratings, reviews, posts, ads, opening hours, photos, page creation dates or "about" text, and nothing is fetched from facebook.com.
  • No page-URL lookup: you search by location, not by a list of page URLs (matching a given URL would mean scanning 80M+ places per run).
  • Most page links are numeric ids (facebook.com/2113811702277065), which open the page as normal; a vanity handle (pageHandle) is filled only when the source link uses one, which is rare (about 0.1% of Lisbon pages). facebookPageId holds the id when the link carries one.
  • Coverage, central Lisbon (release 2026-09-23.1, confidence 0.6 or more): 24,243 places with a Facebook Page link, 23,718 unique pages; of the top 20,000 pages 91% have a phone, 65% a website and 39% a business email (after the personal-email filter); 0.7% list another social profile. Coverage varies by country and is thinner in parts of Africa and Asia.
  • Freshness follows Overture's monthly releases; sourceUpdatedAt shows when the contributing source last updated each row. A closed business or a deleted page can still appear until a source drops it; minConfidence (default 0.6) filters most stale records.
  • One row per page: when several places link the same page (chains, duplicates), only the highest-confidence place is returned and the rest are free. Places whose link is not a page (groups, events, share links) are skipped and free.
  • One query per run, up to 1.5 x 1.5 degrees (about one metro area). For a region or country, run once per city. A city query typically reads 15-45 MB of public GeoParquet in 5-15 seconds at 1 GB memory.
  • Business contacts only: phones, emails and socials are the business's published contacts. Free-mail addresses that do not contain the business name and off-domain personal-looking addresses are removed (counted in droppedPersonalEmails). You remain responsible for how you use the data under GDPR, CAN-SPAM and similar laws.
  • The run's spend limit is respected: the area query is reserved first, pages are charged before delivery, and the run stops cleanly at the limit.

FAQ

How can I find all Facebook business pages in a city?

Enter the city as location, optionally add categories, and run. The actor returns every business in that area whose Overture place record links a Facebook Page, up to 20,000 per run (maxResults). One run covers up to about one metro area.

Does this actor log in to Facebook or scrape facebook.com?

No. It never visits facebook.com, graph.facebook.com or any Meta service and does not use the Graph API. The page links come from the Overture Maps open data, where Meta contributes its places under CDLA-Permissive-2.0.

Why are most page URLs numeric instead of a vanity name?

That is how the links appear in the source data. Numeric links such as facebook.com/2113811702277065 open the page normally, and facebookPageId holds the id. A pageHandle is filled only when the source uses a vanity link, which is rare (about 0.1% of Lisbon pages).

Can I get page likes or follower counts?

No. There are no likes, followers, check-ins, ratings, reviews, posts, ads, opening hours, photos, creation dates or about text. The actor returns contact and location data only.

What share of pages include a phone or email?

In central Lisbon (release 2026-09-23.1), of the top 20,000 pages 91% had a phone, 65% a website and 39% a business email after the personal-email filter. Coverage varies by country and is thinner in parts of Africa and Asia.

How much does it cost?

$0.003 per area query plus $0.004 per page delivered. 50 restaurants in Lisbon cost $0.203 and 1,000 pages cost $4.003. Duplicate places pointing to the same page and rows you filter out are free.
