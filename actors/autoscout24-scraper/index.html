AutoScout24 Car Listings Scraper: 9 EU Sites + AutoTrader.ca

Scrape used and new car listings from AutoScout24 (Germany, Italy, France, Netherlands, Belgium, Austria, Spain, Luxembourg, .com) and AutoTrader.ca: make, model, version, price, mileage, registration, fuel, gearbox, power, dealer, location, photos. No browser. $0.40 per 1,000 listings.

What it does

An AutoScout24 scraper for car listings across Europe and Canada, with no browser and no proxy bill. Paste AutoScout24 search URLs with your filters (make, model, price, year, mileage, fuel, region…) or just type makes and models like "bmw/x5", and get one clean row per car from the public results pages: make, model, model group, version, price and currency, AutoScout24's price rating (top, good, fair price), mileage, first registration and model year, fuel, gearbox, power in kW and hp, engine size, fuel consumption and CO₂, new/used, damage flag, seller type, and for dealers the dealer name, dealer page, office phone and rating, plus postcode and city, photo URLs and the listing URL. It covers autoscout24.de, .it, .fr, .nl, .be, .at, .es, .lu and .com, plus AutoTrader.ca (the same platform), with sort by price, newest offers, mileage or registration. Use it for used car price research and valuation, dealer stock and competitor monitoring, sourcing cars for export or resale, market analysis by model and region, and car datasets for AI agents. Private sellers' names, phones and addresses are never collected. $0.40 per 1,000 listings, with no compute bill on top; problems return a free status row instead of failing.

Limits and honest notes

  • Responsible use: you are responsible for using the data in line with the terms of AutoScout24 and AutoTrader.ca and with data-protection law (such as the GDPR) where you are. This actor is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by AutoScout24 or AutoTrader.ca.
  • It reads only the public results pages (/lst/… and /cars/…), which the sites' robots.txt allow, and checks robots.txt before every page. AutoScout24 disallows /lst?… URLs that have no make in the path; those are refused with a free status row. There is no login, no cookies, no proxies and no CAPTCHA solving; it sends an honest user agent (ActorForge-AutoScout24) and waits at least 1.5 s between pages.
  • Results-page data only (20 cars per page): no full equipment list or description from the detail page. A search shows at most 20 pages (400 cars), so split big searches with price, year or region filters.
  • Personal data is left out: for private sellers there is no name, phone or street (only postcode and city), and dealers' named contact persons are dropped. Dealer company name, dealer page and office phone are business data.
  • If a site answers with a block or a challenge, the run stops at once, keeps the listings already delivered and adds a free status row.
  • Text such as fuel and gearbox is in the site's language (e.g. "Benzin" on .de). Prices are as shown; dealers sometimes enter placeholder prices such as $1.
  • Respects your run budget: set a maximum total charge and the run stops cleanly at the cap, with nothing charged twice. A run with no results returns one free status row.
