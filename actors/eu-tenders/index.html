EU TED Tenders — Public Procurement Notices & Contract Awards

EU public procurement notices from TED (Tenders Electronic Daily) as clean rows: tenders, contract awards with winners and values, prior information and modifications. Filter by CPV, country, keywords, deadline, value, buyer. Monitoring mode returns only new notices. $1.50 per 1,000.

What it does

Every EU public tender and contract award from TED, as clean rows — and only the new ones on each scheduled run. TED (Tenders Electronic Daily) is the official journal of European public procurement: around 3,000 notices every working day from governments, cities, hospitals, utilities and EU institutions in the EU, EEA and beyond. This actor queries the official TED Search API (no key, no browser) and normalises each notice into one row: English and original-language title, notice type (contract notice, contract award, prior information, voluntary ex-ante, modification), publication date, submission deadline (with time), buyer name, country, city and type, CPV codes with English labels (CPV 2008, bundled), main CPV, place of performance (countries and NUTS regions), estimated value and awarded value with currency and an approximate EUR amount, winners (company name, country, size), procedure type, number of lots, tender documents link, description and PDF/XML links. Filter by CPV code or prefix (72* = all IT services), country (ISO 2- or 3-letter), keywords (full text, any language), notice type, publication dates (absolute or '7 days'), deadline still open, minimum value in EUR and buyer name. Turn on monitoring mode and schedule it daily: the actor remembers what it delivered for your exact filters and returns only notices published since the last run — a ready-made tender alert feed for bid teams, B2G sales, CRM and Slack pipelines, market research and competitor award tracking. Simple pricing: $1.50 per 1,000 notices, no compute bill on top; notices already delivered or filtered out are never charged.

Limits and honest notes

  • Source: the official TED Search API v3 of the EU Publications Office (public, no API key). TED publishes one edition of the Official Journal S supplement per working day (Monday to Friday); a notice appears here as soon as TED publishes it.
  • Licence: TED notices can be freely reused for commercial or non-commercial purposes under the European Commission's reuse policy (Commission Decision 2011/833/EU; see ted.europa.eu/en/legal-notice). TED editorial content is CC BY 4.0. Source: TED — Tenders Electronic Daily, © European Union.
  • Fields come from eForms notices (all notices since late 2023). Older TED-schema notices (before 2024) are included but carry fewer structured fields (often no values, deadlines or winners).
  • Values are as published by the buyer. Many buyers do not publish a value (TED returns -1) or enter a placeholder of 0 or 1 — those are returned as null. amountEurApprox converts other currencies at fixed approximate rates and is meant for filtering and sorting, not accounting. On multi-lot awards awardedValue is the notice total, or the sum of the winning tenders.
  • Titles: title is TED's English heading (country – main CPV label – original title); titleOriginal and description are in the notice's original language (truncated to 1,500 characters). TED does not translate free text.
  • Winners are the contractor organisations as published (name, country, size). Contact persons, e-mails, phone numbers and beneficial-owner data are never requested. Sole traders may appear under their trading name, as in the official notice.
  • Monitoring mode keeps one small record per filter combination in the named key-value store 'af-eu-tenders-monitor' in your Apify account (last publication date delivered plus the notice IDs of that day). Changing any filter starts a new monitor; delete the store record to start over. Dates and Max notices are not part of the key.
  • The TED API has no published rate limit; requests are paced to about 2 per second with automatic retries on 429/5xx. Up to 250 notices per request: 1,000 notices take about 10–20 seconds.
  • Respects your run budget: set a maximum total charge and the run stops cleanly at the cap, with every delivered row charged exactly once.
