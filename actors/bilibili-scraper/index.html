What it does

Bilibili (哔哩哔哩, B站) is where much of China's gaming, anime, tech and youth culture lives, but its interface is Chinese-only and its web API needs request signing and passes anti-bot checks. This actor handles that for you and needs no login, no cookies and nothing to configure.

Give it keywords in Chinese or English, specific video URLs, BV ids or av ids, or switch on the popular feed (热门). Each video row includes views, likes, coins (投币), favorites, shares, danmaku (弹幕) count and comment count, plus tags, category, duration, publish time, cover image, the uploader's channel and their follower count. Optionally, includeComments attaches a sample of top and newest comments, anonymised to text, time and likes.

It is used for China influencer and KOL discovery, brand and campaign monitoring, competitor content research and gaming or anime trend analysis. Videos cost $4 per 1,000 and anonymised comments $0.50 per 1,000. Text comes back in its original language, mostly Simplified Chinese, and is not translated.

How it works

  1. Keyword searches run against Bilibili's own search with your chosen sortBy order, up to maxVideos per term (Bilibili caps search at 1,000 results per term).
  2. Each search hit is enriched with a per-video detail lookup to add coins, shares, tags and the uploader's follower count.
  3. Video URLs, BV ids and av ids in videoUrls are fetched directly; deleted, private or region-locked videos are skipped and never charged.
  4. With includePopular on, the current popular feed is added, again up to maxVideos.
  5. With includeComments on, about 60 top and newest comments per video (including replies) are attached, with commenter names, IDs and avatars removed.

Limits and honest notes

  • Public data only, no login: videos, stats and channel info that any visitor can see.
  • Comments are a SAMPLE, not the full thread. Without a login Bilibili shows guests only a few top and newest comment threads, about 60 comments per video including replies. Commenter names, user IDs and avatars are never returned, only text, time and likes.
  • Subtitles/CC tracks and the ranking chart (排行榜) need a logged-in account on Bilibili, so they are not included. Use the popular feed for trending videos.
  • Keyword search returns up to 1,000 results per term (Bilibili's own cap: 50 pages × 20).
  • Search rows are enriched with a per-video detail lookup. If that lookup fails, the video is still delivered with search-level stats (coins, shares and uploader followers are null).
  • Deleted, private or region-locked video URLs/BV ids are skipped and never charged.
  • Text is returned in its original language (mostly Simplified Chinese) and is not translated.
  • Respects your run budget: set a maximum total charge and the actor stops cleanly at the cap.

FAQ

Can I scrape Bilibili without logging in?

Yes. The actor uses only public data any visitor can see: videos, stats and channel info. It needs no account, no cookies and no configuration.

How many Bilibili search results can I get per keyword?

Up to 1,000 per term, which is Bilibili's own cap (50 pages of 20). Set maxVideos to control how many you take; it ranges from 1 to 1,000.

Does it return all comments on a Bilibili video?

No. Without a login Bilibili shows guests only a few top and newest threads, so you get a sample of about 60 comments per video including replies. Only text, time and likes are returned; commenter names, user IDs and avatars never are.

What are coins and danmaku in the output?

Coins (投币) are a Bilibili-specific way viewers reward a video, and danmaku (弹幕) are the comments that scroll over the video while it plays. Both are returned per video alongside views, likes, favorites and shares.

Are titles translated into English?

No. Titles, tags and comments are returned in their original language, mostly Simplified Chinese. Pass them to your own translator or LLM if you need English.
