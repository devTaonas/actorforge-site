AI News Brief Agent — Cited Market & Company Intelligence

Name a topic, company or market; the agent searches recent global news, groups stories, reads key articles and writes a cited AI brief: summary, dated developments, companies, sentiment, risks, opportunities and sources. $0.05 per brief + AI tokens at cost.

Run on Apify →See pricing

What it does

Get a cited intelligence brief on any topic, company or market from the last 1–7 days of global news, with no reading required. Type topics such as *"Nvidia"*, *"EV batteries Europe"* or *"UK fintech regulation"*. The agent scans recent online news worldwide (GDELT's open news data: tens of thousands of outlets in 65+ languages, refreshed every 15 minutes), removes syndicated duplicates, groups the coverage into storylines, reads the lead of the most important articles from different outlets, and has an AI analyst write one structured brief per topic:

- Executive summary: what happened and why it matters, in 3–5 sentences - Key developments, each with a date, a short headline, a summary, its sentiment and the source URLs that support it - Companies, public figures and places mentioned - Overall sentiment (positive / neutral / negative / mixed, score −1 to 1, with a one-line rationale) - Risks, opportunities and what to watch next, each tied to its sources - Storylines (clusters of related coverage, with outlet counts), the full source list, and optionally one row per article (outlet, country, dates, storyline, read or not, cited or not)

Limits and honest notes

  • AI tokens are a second, separate charge on your own Apify account, made by apify/openrouter at OpenRouter's rates (10× on the Apify free plan). They are not part of this actor's per-brief price and are not limited by the run's maximum total charge, so use maxLlmCostUsd (default $0.10) to cap them. llmCostUsd is an estimate from the usage OpenRouter reports; your Apify billing page is authoritative.
  • News discovery uses the GDELT Project's open news data (credited in every brief's attribution field): the 15-minute files of newly seen articles (about 4,000 English and 16,000 translated articles per hour), sampled evenly over your time window (maxNewsFiles), plus GDELT's full-text search API when it answers within about 30 seconds. Topics are matched in headlines and URLs, so a story that never names your topic in its headline may be missed; niche topics may need more news files or a longer window. GDELT covers a very large share of online news, but few paywall-only or social-media sources.
  • A topic with fewer than 3 matching headlines gets a free headlines-only row instead of a charged AI brief (no AI tokens are spent), with tips to broaden it.
  • Articles are read as static HTML (no browser), robots.txt is respected per site (RFC 9309), and requests identify themselves honestly as ActorForge-NewsBrief. Paywalled, blocked or JavaScript-only pages are skipped, and their headlines are still used. Only the lead of each article is read, and no article text is ever output.
  • Copyright: summaries are written in the AI's own words. A quote is kept only when it is one sentence found word for word in an article the agent read, at most one per article, always with its source URL.
  • AI analysts can be wrong. Every claim must cite the articles it comes from and uncited claims are removed automatically, but check important facts at the source. The brief reflects how the news covered the topic, which is not necessarily the truth.
  • People: the AI is told to list only public figures (executives, officials, politicians, spokespeople). The actor is not designed for researching or profiling private individuals.
  • Short words (UK, EU, AI, EV) are matched as whole words in headlines and URLs. A source-country filter is applied by the outlet's country domain (.co.uk, .de…) in the news files, so outlets on .com domains are not counted for a country.
  • Page text is treated as data: the AI is told to ignore instructions inside articles (prompt-injection defence), but no defence is perfect.

Use it from code

Run the actor through the Apify API with the input above.

cURL

curl -X POST "https://api.apify.com/v2/acts/actor-forge~news-market-brief-agent/run-sync-get-dataset-items" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $APIFY_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"topics": ["electric vehicles"], "lookbackHours": 48, "maxHeadlines": 30, "maxArticlesToRead": 4, "maxNewsFiles": 24}'

JavaScript (Node.js)

// Node.js 18+ (built-in fetch). Set APIFY_TOKEN in your environment.
const input = {
  "topics": [
    "electric vehicles"
  ],
  "lookbackHours": 48,
  "maxHeadlines": 30,
  "maxArticlesToRead": 4,
  "maxNewsFiles": 24
};

const res = await fetch(
  "https://api.apify.com/v2/acts/actor-forge~news-market-brief-agent/run-sync-get-dataset-items",
  {
    method: "POST",
    headers: {
      Authorization: `Bearer ${process.env.APIFY_TOKEN}`,
      "Content-Type": "application/json",
    },
    body: JSON.stringify(input),
  }
);
const items = await res.json();
console.log(items.length, items[0]);

Python

# pip install apify-client   (set APIFY_TOKEN in your environment)
import os
from apify_client import ApifyClient

client = ApifyClient(os.environ["APIFY_TOKEN"])
run_input = {
    "topics": [
        "electric vehicles"
    ],
    "lookbackHours": 48,
    "maxHeadlines": 30,
    "maxArticlesToRead": 4,
    "maxNewsFiles": 24
}

run = client.actor("actor-forge/news-market-brief-agent").call(run_input=run_input)
for item in client.dataset(run["defaultDatasetId"]).iterate_items():
    print(item)

The run-sync-get-dataset-items endpoint waits for the run (up to 5 minutes) and returns the dataset rows directly. For longer runs, start the run asynchronously and read the dataset when it finishes, as the Python example does. Replace APIFY_TOKEN with your own token from the Apify console.
